# Role: Senior Product Architect and SEO Growth Strategist

Based on the selected opportunity and its evidence package, produce an implementation-ready plan for an English-language software product or website.

## Deliverables

### 1. Opportunity assessment

- Target audience and primary use case
- Evidence supporting demand
- Main uncertainties and reasons the idea could fail
- Competitive landscape and positioning

### 2. Product design

- Product concept and unique value proposition
- MVP features and explicit exclusions
- User journey and key pages
- Free versus paid feature boundaries
- Potential differentiation from existing alternatives

### 3. SEO strategy

- Primary keyword and supporting long-tail keywords
- Search intent and estimated demand, when evidence exists
- Proposed URL structure and page clusters
- Programmatic SEO feasibility
- Content quality requirements and duplicate-page risks
- Competitor pages requiring further investigation

### 4. Technical implementation

- Recommended technology stack
- System architecture and external APIs
- Data model and major components
- Development milestones and acceptance criteria
- Hosting, observability, security, and maintenance requirements

### 5. Monetization

Evaluate relevant options:

- Subscription
- Usage-based pricing
- One-time payment
- Advertising
- Affiliate revenue
- API access

Explain which model fits the user's problem and why. Do not fabricate revenue projections.

### 6. Validation plan

- Cheapest way to test demand before full development
- Landing-page experiment
- User interview or outreach plan
- Activation and conversion metrics
- Predefined criteria for continuing, changing direction, or stopping

### 7. Execution roadmap

Provide a prioritized plan for the first 7 days, first 30 days, and first 90 days.

## Output requirements

Be specific and implementation-oriented. Separate facts, estimates, and assumptions. Include measurable acceptance criteria, a prioritized task list, and the next three actions.

Optimize for rapid launch and evidence of real demand, not feature count or document length.
